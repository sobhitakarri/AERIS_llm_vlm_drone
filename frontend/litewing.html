    Motor Test (ground, no flight)

    Spins all four motors together at a low thrust for a few seconds. Raw thrust goes through the firmware's command watchdog, so the motors stop within 0.5 s if the link drops. Individual motors cannot be isolated this way: with props off, listen and look to see which one does not turn.

    Needs Debug Mode unticked (debug never sends a motor command). Thrust max 15 %, max 3 s: it cannot lift the drone. STOP or E-STOP ends it at once.
